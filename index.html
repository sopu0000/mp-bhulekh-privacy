1. In short

  • There are no accounts, logins or payments in the app.
  • The developer runs no server. What you search for goes from your phone straight to the government portal, and the developer never receives it.
  • Records you save stay on your phone.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or phone calls.

2. What you search for, and where it goes

To find a record you enter details such as district, tehsil and village, khasra or plot number, a landowner's name or a ULPIN. The app sends these details over a secure (HTTPS) connection directly from your phone to the MP Bhulekh WebGIS portal at webgis2.mpbhulekh.gov.in, which returns the record. To do this the app opens a temporary guest session with the portal, the same kind of session the portal's own website uses. No name, phone number or other personal detail of yours is needed.

The developer does not operate any server and does not receive, see or store your searches. The portal is run by the Government of Madhya Pradesh, and its own terms and privacy practices apply to the requests it receives.

3. Land record information shown in the app

Land records are public records published by the portal. They can include landowners' names, khasra and khata numbers, area, land type and similar details. The app only displays what the portal returns. It does not collect these records into any database of its own or send them anywhere else.

4. Information stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, so it can work faster and offline:

  • Records you choose to save, with the date they were saved.
  • Your recent searches.
  • Your settings, such as language and theme.
  • Cached lists of districts, tehsils and villages, and map data for villages you have opened.
  • PDF copies you open or download (Khasra, Khatauni and Naksha specimen copies). If you choose to save a PDF to your phone, it is placed in your Downloads folder.
  • Cookies set by the websites you open inside the app's built-in browser.

None of this is uploaded to the developer. You can delete saved records and PDFs on the app's Saved Records screen. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything in its private storage. PDFs you saved to Downloads stay there until you delete them.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, and from it your approximate location,
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and language,
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

On recent Android versions, the ads software may also use Android's ad services (Privacy Sandbox topics and attribution). Google handles this data under its own privacy policy. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your ad choices

On most Android phones you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads). You can manage Android's ad services under Settings → Security & privacy → Privacy → Ads privacy. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

6. Other services the app connects to

Each of the services below receives the normal technical details of a web request, such as your IP address and the address being requested, and is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Map tiles: OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org, see the OSMF privacy policy) and Esri satellite imagery (server.arcgisonline.com, see Esri's privacy statement). They learn which map area is being displayed.
  • Map software: the map component is loaded from the unpkg.com content network.
  • Google Maps: only if you tap the option to open a location in Google Maps.
  • Official websites that you choose to open, such as mpbhulekh.gov.in, rcms.mp.gov.in, samagra.gov.in, services.ecourts.gov.in and cmhelpline.mp.gov.in.

The app never handles payments. The Revenue Payment option opens the official portal in your phone's own browser, and any payment you make there happens on the government website.

7. What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, SMS or call logs.
  • It does not sell, rent or share personal information, because the developer does not hold any.
  • The in-app help assistant shows built-in answers on your phone and does not send anything anywhere.
